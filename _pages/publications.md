My work examines how early care and education (ECE) policies shape access to care, program quality, and the educator workforce. I conduct quantitative research and work with state and local partners to produce evidence that can inform ECE policy.

## Early childhood research

### <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2026.03.003" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Supporting newly hired child care teachers: The role of onboarding</a>

New child care teachers receive widely varying levels of onboarding support. In this study with Anna Markowitz and Daphna Bassok, we examine how those experiences relate to teachers' preparation, job satisfaction, professional well-being, and risk of turnover. *Early Childhood Research Quarterly*, 2026. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2026.03.003" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View article</a>

### <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2024.05.007" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Where has all the time gone? Describing time use in full- vs. half-day pre-kindergarten</a>

Using repeated observations of pre-K classrooms, Allison Atteberry and I examine how full- and half-day school-based programs allocate time across instructional content, classroom activities, and non-instructional routines. *Early Childhood Research Quarterly*, 2024. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ecresq.2024.05.007" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">View article</a>
